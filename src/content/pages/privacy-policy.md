**Last Updated:** October 2026

At **TechNKT** (accessible from https://technkt.in), your privacy and data security are central to everything we build. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect, how it is used, and the steps we take to ensure your personal details and order information remain safe.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

When you interact with TechNKT — whether purchasing a digital subscription, downloading an ebook, or inquiring about our website development services — we may collect:

- **Contact Details:** Your name, email address, and phone or WhatsApp number provided during checkout or consultation inquiries.
- **Order Information:** Details of the products, subscriptions, or web development packages you select, transaction identifiers, and delivery timestamps.
- **Client Project Assets:** For custom website development clients, project requirements, brand assets, design preferences, and feedback provided during the development lifecycle.
- **Technical & Usage Data:** Basic diagnostic data such as browser type, IP address, device information, and interaction timestamps to ensure platform stability and prevent fraudulent activity.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely for legitimate business purposes, including:

1. **Order Fulfillment:** Generating and delivering your digital subscription credentials, combo access, and ebook downloads.
2. **Customer Support:** Resolving any technical or credential queries, replacement warranty requests, and providing prompt assistance via email or WhatsApp.
3. **Web Development Execution:** Planning, developing, deploying, and maintaining custom websites as agreed in client proposals.
4. **Platform Security:** Detecting, preventing, and addressing fraudulent transactions or abuse of our digital services.

---

### 3. Payment Processing & Financial Security

All monetary transactions on TechNKT are processed through certified, PCI-DSS compliant third-party payment gateways (such as Stripe and verified payment partners). 

- TechNKT does **not** collect, store, or have direct access to your credit card numbers, CVVs, or online banking passwords.
- All checkout communication is encrypted over modern Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).

---

### 4. Digital Account Credentials & Warranty Privacy

For subscriptions that involve managed account credentials:

- Login details generated for you are kept strictly private and transmitted directly to your registered email or dashboard.
- Under our replacement warranty, any reported access issues are addressed directly with replacement credentials without exposing user personal data.

---

### 5. Cookies & Local Storage

TechNKT uses lightweight browser storage (such as `localStorage`) solely for:
- Maintaining your active shopping cart (`technkt_cart`) across sessions.
- Remembering interface preferences (such as audio, motion, and theme settings).

We do not use invasive third-party cross-site tracking cookies.

---

### 6. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We share data only with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our website, conducting our business, or servicing you, provided those parties agree to keep this information confidential.

---

### 7. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
- Request correction of any inaccurate or incomplete information.
- Request deletion of your contact data from our active customer records.

---
