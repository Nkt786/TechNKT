**Last Updated:** October 2026

At **TechNKT**, customer confidence and transparent dealings are our highest priorities. Because we offer instant-delivery digital products as well as bespoke website engineering services, our refund and replacement policy is designed to be fair, prompt, and crystal clear.

---

### 1. Digital Subscriptions: Replacement Warranty First

For digital entertainment passes, streaming profiles, software tools, and subscription combos:

- **100% Replacement Guarantee:** Every subscription plan comes with full replacement warranty coverage for the entirety of its purchased period (1 month, 3 months, 6 months, or 1 year).
- **If You Encounter an Issue:** If your credentials cease working, password changes occur upstream, or an error arises, simply message us on WhatsApp or email `support@technkt.com` with your Order ID.
- **Resolution Time:** We typically inspect and provide replacement working credentials within **2 to 4 hours** (and guaranteed within 24 hours).
- **Refund Eligibility:** If our team is unable to provide working replacement credentials within 24 hours for a valid unexpired subscription, you are entitled to a pro-rated or full refund back to your original payment method.

---

### 2. Ebooks & Digital Downloads

- Once an ebook, PDF guide, or downloadable digital asset is accessed or downloaded, sales are generally final due to the non-returnable nature of digital content.
- If you experience a broken file, corrupted download link, or duplicate transaction charge, our support team will immediately rectify the link or issue a prompt refund for the duplicate charge.

---

### 3. Website Development Services

For custom website design, web app development, and landing page projects:

- **Initial Consultation & Deposit:** The initial discovery phase and kickoff milestone ensure alignment before engineering begins.
- **Milestone Approvals:** Client projects are broken into clear milestones (Design Approval, Development & Integration, Final Review & Launch).
- **Cancellations:** If a client chooses to cancel a project prior to development commencing, any unspent milestone funds minus discovery hours will be refunded. Once a milestone is signed off and delivered, that milestone payment is non-refundable.

---

### 4. How to Request a Replacement or Refund

To initiate a warranty claim or refund request:

1. Send an email to **support@technkt.com** or chat directly with our WhatsApp support line.
2. Provide your **Order ID** or invoice number and a brief screenshot or description of the issue.
3. Our support agent will verify and resolve your request without unnecessary hassle.
